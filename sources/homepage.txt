07 / FREQUENT QUERIES
QUESTIONS?
START HERE
Who can take part?
Developers, designers and people with ideas for the NEAR ecosystem. No wallet connection is needed to submit: you only enter a NEAR account address, and if you win, the prize is sent there.
Do I need a team already?
No. Build as a solo builder or with a team — one submission per project, and one person can enter up to 2 different projects, if they are independent and each README names the other one. Looking for teammates? Join the NEAR Discord and find them there.
When does it happen?
The hackathon starts 1 Nov 2026 · 00:00 UTC. Development runs for two weeks and ends 14 Nov 2026 · 23:59 UTC, and results are announced by 18 Nov 2026 · 23:59 UTC. The full schedule is in the at-a-glance panel.
What should we submit?
A working prototype in a public repository and a public demo post on X: say you built it at NEAR HACKATHON and attach the demo video or screenshots. Send both links through the form before development ends, 14 Nov 2026 · 23:59 UTC.
How will projects be judged?
Claude Code & Codex screen every project first: they check that it runs and does what the README says. The human judges above make the decisions. Previous NEAR hackathons evaluated technical implementation, design, impact, originality and sustainability — a good guide to prepare by.
Can I build something across multiple tracks?
Yes. Select every track your project fits in the submission form. It is still one submission per project.
